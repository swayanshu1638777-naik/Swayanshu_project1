# Smart College Portal

Open `index.html` to explore the SSIPMT campus portal. The responsive landing
page links to student, faculty, and administration spaces, as well as sign-in
and student registration. Its full-screen temple world is rendered locally
from `assets/temple/temple-world.html` and its companion Three.js assets; the
landing-page scroll adds a restrained camera drift, keeping the background
focused on a single calm temple scene.
